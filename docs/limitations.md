# Limitations and responsible use

- Scenarios are synthetic and cannot represent every enterprise environment.
- Reference expectations are concise anchors, not exhaustive runbooks.
- Product capabilities, licensing and interfaces change; verify current documentation.
- Scores are reviewer judgments, affected by expertise and bias.
- The CLI validates input and calculates arithmetic; it does not understand or fact-check answers.
- Sample reviews are illustrative workflow examples, not empirical benchmark results.
- Never submit customer logs, personal data, credentials, secrets or proprietary incident details.
- Do not execute generated commands in production; validate in an authorised isolated lab.
